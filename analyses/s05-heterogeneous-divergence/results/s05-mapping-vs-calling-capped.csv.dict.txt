One row per (caller, iteration, local-D bin). Alt-evidence threshold --alt-min = 2 reads.

Fields:
  caller, iteration : condition
  window            : local-D window half-size (bp) used for binning
  locald_lo/hi/mid  : local-divergence bin edges + midpoint (sites/bp)
  n_sites           : divergent truth SNPs sampled in this bin
  n_recovered/calling/mapping : site counts by class
  frac_recovered    : recovered / n_sites (== recall)
  frac_calling      : CALLING-limited (alt present >= alt-min but uncalled) / n
  frac_mapping      : MAPPING-limited (alt absent at p) / n
  mean_f_alt        : mean (ex_alt+sh_alt)/n_true_alt = alt-support survival
                      (low where mapping-limited: alt reads did not arrive)
  mean_clipped_frac : mean fraction of alt-carrying reads that arrived but
                      were soft-clipped over p
  mean_mismapped_frac : mean fraction mismapped/diff-chr/unmapped
  mean_alt_p/depth_p: mean alt-supporting reads / total depth in the pileup at p

frac_recovered + frac_calling + frac_mapping = 1 within each bin.

KEY READING
-----------
Even on this realistic capped config the FULL transition is present: at low local D the loss is
gatk CALLING-limited (mapping ~0); the calling band peaks near local D ~0.145 (gatk frac_calling
~0.46) and MAPPING-limited then takes over (~0.80 of sites by local D 0.225). The calling->mapping
crossover (~0.16) matches the uncapped characterization config almost exactly, confirming the
mechanism is a property of the caller + read length, not of the field configuration. The realistic
mass sits at local D <= 0.20 (dashed line in the figure); the bins past it are sparse (n_sites
1640 at 0.225, 67 at 0.275) but still trace the wall's onset.
