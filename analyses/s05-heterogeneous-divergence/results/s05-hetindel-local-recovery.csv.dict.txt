Data dictionary for analyses/s05-heterogeneous-divergence/results/s05-hetindel-local-recovery.csv

s05 het-indels: SNP and indel recovery binned by local divergence.
One row per (caller, iteration, vtype, local-D bin). Window = +/-150 bp.

Fields:
  caller, iteration : condition (mean divergence fixed at 0.06)
  vtype             : snp | indel (both GT 1|1 divergence truth sites)
  locald_lo/hi/mid  : local-divergence bin edges + midpoint (div-SNP density)
  n_sites           : truth sites of this vtype in the bin
  n_recovered       : matched (POS,REF,ALT) in the accumulated recovered VCF
  recall            : n_recovered / n_sites

Overlay against s02 homogeneous recall-vs-mean-divergence: het recall at
local divergence x below s02 recall at mean divergence x => super-additive.
