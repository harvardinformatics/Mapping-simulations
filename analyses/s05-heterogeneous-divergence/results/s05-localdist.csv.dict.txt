Sites: each config's FIXED-divergence (1|1) truth SNPs, with local divergence from
its mm39-truth-locald.tsv (annotate_local_divergence.py). Configs: L20000-S0.0, L2000-S1.0, L20000-S1.0, L200000-S1.0, L20000-S2.0.

Histogram of per-site local divergence; one row per (config, window, bin).

Fields:
  field/corr_len/sigma : divergence-field config (as above)
  window     : local-D window half-size in bp (150 = read scale, 500 = fragment)
  locald_mid : histogram bin midpoint; bins are 0.01 wide from 0 to 0.6, plus a
               '>0.6' overflow row
  n_sites    : truth divergent SNP sites with local divergence in that bin

The homogeneous control (sigma=0) concentrates near the mean; larger sigma
spreads sites across a wide range of local divergence (the heterogeneity).
