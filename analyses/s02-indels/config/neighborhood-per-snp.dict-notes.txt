WHAT THIS IS
------------
Per divergent-SNP, per-iteration measure of the local "assemblable neighbourhood": how many nearby
truth variants are STILL UNRECOVERED (not yet called into the consensus) around each divergent SNP.
The premise (see the tool docstring): gatk's reference-anchored reassembler fails while the local
sequence is too divergent from the (consensus) reference; as iteration bakes neighbouring variants
into the consensus, the local unrecovered load falls and the site becomes callable.

The *_indel columns mirror the SNP columns so the notebook can contrast the neighbour SNP-vs-indel
unrecovered load across iterations (synthesis §5 "Indels").

KEY READING (s02, pooled fraction still unrecovered = sum(unrec)/sum(neighbor_truth), D = 0.06-0.10)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At every caller and divergence the indel unrecovered-fraction sits ABOVE the SNP one: iteration
clears indels LESS completely than SNPs, leaving a larger residual (~15-47% of neighbour indels vs
~2-30% of SNPs still unrecovered by iteration 3). Strongly caller-dependent: bcftools recovers almost
no indels single-shot (78-95% unrecovered at iter 1) then iteration rescues it sharply (15-32% by
iter 3); freebayes stays stuck (30-47% at iter 3); gatk improves modestly (52% -> 40% at D = 0.10).
Iteration reduces the indel load but cannot eliminate it — that residual is the divergence it cannot
bake into the consensus.
