Note        : Corrected method (2026-10-26). Earlier versions of this table came from reading bases through mason's -oa CIGAR, which writes dense SNP clusters as fake 1D..1I gap pairs; that wrongly flagged tens of thousands of SNPs as phantoms (still counted here as n_cigar_flag_*). Bases are now read at each read's haplotype position (oP).

Method: both haplotypes rebuilt from data/s02-indels/sim/30X/19/0.08/0.005/42/mm39-truth.vcf.gz (the mason-format truth VCF); each truth read's base
at a SNP is SEQ[h - oP] (h = the SNP's position on the read's haplotype oH; oP = the read's
haplotype start, from data/s02-indels/sim/30X/19/0.08/0.005/42/mm39-truth.oa.bam). A site is judged from CONSISTENT reads of the haplotypes that
carry its alt (both for 1|1, one for a het): reads whose mismatches against their haplotype,
away from that haplotype's truth SNPs, do not exceed mason's error count XE. Phantom = depth
>= 5 and alt fraction < 0.10; depth < 5 = low coverage (not judged).

One row (this region / divergence / seed). The first 12 columns keep the meaning they
had in earlier versions; rates are NA when their denominator is 0.

Fields:
  region, divergence, seed : condition labels
  n_truth_snp       : biallelic truth SNPs (div + het)
  n_truth_div, n_truth_het : of those, 1|1 / heterozygous
  n_phantom_div, n_phantom_het : phantom SNPs, by zygosity
  phantom_div_rate  : n_phantom_div / n_truth_div
  phantom_het_rate  : n_phantom_het / n_truth_het
  n_lowcov          : SNPs with depth < 5 (not judged)
  mean_alt_frac     : mean alt fraction over the judged SNPs
  n_lowcov_div, n_lowcov_het : n_lowcov by zygosity
  min_alt_frac      : lowest alt fraction of any judged SNP
  n_af_0.10_0.50, n_af_0.50_0.90, n_af_ge_0.90 : judged SNPs by alt-fraction bin
  refhap_alt_frac   : at het SNPs, alt fraction among consistent reads of the haplotype
                      WITHOUT the alt (should be about the sequencing-error rate)
  n_reads           : truth reads scanned
  frac_reads_exact  : reads whose mismatches against their haplotype equal XE exactly
  frac_reads_consistent : reads used for scoring (see Method)
  frac_reads_seq_indel  : inconsistent reads explained by one 1-2 bp shift (a mason
                      sequencing indel); not scored
  frac_reads_unexplained : the remaining inconsistent reads (tool fails above 0.0010)
  max_window_unexplained : highest unexplained fraction of any 10000-bp haplotype window
                      with >= 100 reads (tool fails above 0.05)
  n_cigar_flag_div, n_cigar_flag_het : SNPs the OLD method (bases read through the -oa
                      CIGAR, all reads pooled) flags with the same thresholds
  n_cigar_flag_now_phantom : of those, phantom under the corrected method
  hap1_len, hap2_len : rebuilt haplotype lengths (checked against ref + inserted - deleted)
