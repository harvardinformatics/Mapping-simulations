Data dictionary: neighborhood-summary.csv
Produced by analyses/s01-baseline-iterative/scripts/neighborhood_assembly.py

For each sampled true hom-div SNP p and each consecutive iteration pair (k -> k+1), the number of
OTHER true hom-div SNPs within +/-window_bp of p that are NOT yet recovered (not in the consensus)
is counted -- the local mismatch load gatk's reference-anchored reassembler faces. Rows are means
over the SNPs of one (caller, divergence, fate) group.

caller                  : variant caller (gatk | bcftools | freebayes)
divergence              : simulated hom-div divergence D
fate                    : transition class over the pair -- flip (not-called->called),
                          stayed_notcalled, stayed_called, lost (called->not-called)
window_bp               : half-window W (bp) around p (neighbors counted in [p-W, p+W], excluding p)
n                       : number of (SNP, pair) instances in the group
mean_neighbor_truth     : mean # of neighbor true hom-div SNPs in the window (the local true divergence)
mean_unrecovered_before : mean # of those neighbors NOT yet recovered at iteration k (before)
mean_unrecovered_after  : mean # NOT yet recovered at iteration k+1 (after)
