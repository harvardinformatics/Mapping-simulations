s07 SV-confounder, false-positive side: do SVs also CREATE false calls near their
breakpoints? One row per caller, over the RAW single-shot (iter1) normalized call VCF
(<caller>/.../iter1/mm39_calls.norm.vcf.gz), SNP divergence background = 0.02.
A false positive (FP) is a called SNP/indel whose (POS, REF, ALT) matches no truth
site in truth.norm (SV records excluded). Breakpoints as in the main table.

Fields:
  caller                    : variant caller (gatk | bcftools | freebayes)
  n_fp                      : FP calls on the contig
  frac_fp_within_150bp       : fraction of those FPs within 150 bp of a breakpoint
  genomic_frac_within_150bp  : fraction of the contig within 150 bp of a breakpoint,
                              approximated as n_breakpoints * 2 * 150 / contig length
                              (breakpoints are sparse, so windows ~never overlap)
  enrichment                : frac_fp_within / genomic_frac_within; 1 = no excess
                              of FPs near breakpoints, > 1 = SVs create false calls
A caller is absent when its iter1 call VCF does not exist or it has no FPs.
